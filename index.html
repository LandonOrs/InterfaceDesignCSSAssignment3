My favorite candy is Sour Patch Kids. They are a beloved candy that combines a sour taste with a chewy texture, making them a favorite among many.

The Sour-Then-Sweet Candy

Sour Patch Kids were invented in the early 1970s by Frank Galatolie, originally under the name “Mars Men,” before being rebranded as Sour Patch Kids in 1985.

Sour Patch Kids products offer a unique twist on classic favorites, combining the beloved “sour then sweet” experience with innovative flavors and formats.
